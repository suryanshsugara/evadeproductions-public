# Evade Productions: Beat Vault

![Beat Vault home page](docs/home.png)

**Live site:** [evadeproductions.com](https://evadeproductions.com)

Beat Vault is the home of Evade Productions, a producer storefront where artists can discover, preview and license original beats, request custom production, and get their files delivered fast.

> This repository is a public showcase. The source code is private and all rights are reserved.

## What you can do

- **Browse and preview beats.** Search the catalog and listen to a short preview of every beat before you decide.
- **License instantly.** Pick the license tier that fits your release and pay securely. Your files and license are ready as soon as payment clears.
- **Exclusive rights.** Buy a beat exclusively and it is taken off the catalog for everyone else.
- **Full-Catalog Listening Pass.** A monthly membership that lets you stream complete tracks across the catalog to audition hooks and arrangements. It is listening only and does not include downloads or commercial rights.
- **Stems and project files.** Higher license tiers include extra deliverables, delivered within 48 hours of purchase.
- **Custom production.** Send an enquiry for a beat made for you, review a quote and pay online.
- **Your account.** Keep your purchases, licenses and downloads in one place, with fresh download links whenever you need them.
- **Support.** Signed-in customers can open a support ticket, follow replies and get email updates that link back to their ticket.

## Built for trust

- Clear license terms for every tier, with a verifiable license for each purchase.
- Secure checkout and private file delivery.
- Verified accounts for support, so your conversations stay yours.
